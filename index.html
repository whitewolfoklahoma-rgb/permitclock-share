Help

The short version of everything PermitClock does.

How do I add a credential?

On the Credentials tab, use the "Add a credential" form — kind, name, and expiration date are enough to put it on the clock. Add the number, jurisdiction, holder, and renewal cost so your renewal packets come out pre-filled.

What do the statuses mean?

Current — more than 90 days out. Due in 90 / 30 / 7 — inside that chase window. Renewal filed — you marked the renewal submitted; the clock keeps watching until the new date is entered. Lapsed — the date has passed with no renewal filed. Lapsed is the only status that should ever surprise you — the chase ladder exists so it doesn't.

How does the permit review clock work?

When you add a permit, enter the date you applied and the review window the jurisdiction promises, in business days. PermitClock counts business days (Monday–Friday) since you applied; the day the count passes the promised window, the permit is flagged past its review window on the Permits tab and in the weekly digest — that's your cue to call the city with the permit number in hand.

What is a renewal packet?

On the Renewal Packets tab, pick any credential and PermitClock assembles the packet from your stored records: the credential's number, holder, jurisdiction, expiration, and renewal cost, plus the checklist for that kind of renewal. Print it, work the checklist, sign, and submit it yourself — PermitClock prepares paperwork; it never files or signs for you.

What is the compliance register?

One dated file from the Dashboard — CSV or print — listing every credential, its number, jurisdiction, holder, expiration, and status. It is the document an insurer, GC, or bonding company asks for when they want proof everything is current.

Where does my data live? How do I back it up?

In this demo edition, everything lives on this device, in this browser — no account, nothing uploaded. Export the compliance register CSV to keep a dated copy, and use Reset below to restore the fictional sample data or Clear to empty the app. Clearing your browser's site data erases the vault, so export before you do.
